## 1. Unscrew the four small screws and remove the back cover

## 2. Use five screws to mount servo 6 on the coupling

## 3. Click center calibration in the Feetech host tool (keep the gripper closed!)

**Download the Feetech servo debugging tool**

- Windows PC

https://gitee.com/ftservo/fddebug

Download [`FD1.9.8.5(250729).7z`](https://gitee.com/ftservo/fddebug/blob/master/FD1.9.8.5(250729).7z), extract it, and run the exe program inside

- Ubuntu PC

https://github.com/Kotakku/FT_SCServo_Debug_Qt

1. Open the Feetech host debugging tool, select the COM port, set the baud rate to one million, and click "Open"
2. Click "Search"; once "STS3215" appears, click "Stop" and then click "STS3215"
3. Select "Program" at the top
4. Click "Center calibration"

## 4. Align the cylinder and fit the back cover

## 5. Screw the four small screws back in

## 6. Use four small screws to install the servo mounting bracket

## 7. Use 6 small screws to install the servo mounting bracket that can hold the camera bracket

## 8. Use 4 washer screws to mount the camera on the bracket

## 9. Use 2 small screws to connect the camera bracket to the servo mounting bracket

## 10. Install the adapter part on servo 5's servo horn

## 11. Connect the adapter part to the parallel-jaw gripper
